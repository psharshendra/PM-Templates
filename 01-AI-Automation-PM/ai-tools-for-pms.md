# 🤖 AI Tools for Project Managers

A curated guide to AI tools that supercharge PM productivity across planning, communication, reporting and delivery.

---

## 🗂️ Planning & Scheduling

| Tool | Use Case | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Claude (Anthropic) | Draft project charters, risk registers, stakeholder comms | Deep thinking & long documents |
| ChatGPT | Brainstorming, meeting summaries, scope docs | Quick ideation |
| Notion AI | In-doc summarisation & action item extraction | Teams already on Notion |
| GitHub Copilot | Automating repetitive repo tasks | Tech-forward PM teams |
