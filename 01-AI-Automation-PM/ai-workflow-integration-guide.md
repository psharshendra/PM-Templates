# 🔄 AI Workflow Integration Guide for PMs

How to embed AI tools into your daily PM workflow without disrupting your team.

## 🌅 Daily AI-Assisted PM Routine

| Time | Activity | AI Tool | Time Saved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | Summarise overnight emails & Teams messages | Copilot for M365 | 30 mins |
| Standup | Extract blockers & action items from meeting | Fireflies.ai | 20 mins |
| Afternoon | Draft status update & stakeholder comms | Claude / ChatGPT | 45 mins |
| EOD | Log risks & update issue register | Claude / ChatGPT | 20 mins |

## 🚀 AI Adoption Roadmap for Your Team

### Phase 1 — Individual (Week 1-2)
- [ ] Identify your top 3 repetitive tasks consuming 2+ hours/week
- [ ] Start using Claude or ChatGPT for status reports & emails
- [ ] Build your personal prompt library

### Phase 2 — Team Pilot (Week 3-4)
- [ ] Introduce Fireflies.ai for standup transcription
- [ ] Share prompt library with the team
- [ ] Run a 30-min AI tools demo session with your team

### Phase 3 — Scale (Month 2)
- [ ] Integrate Copilot for M365 across the team
- [ ] Connect Azure DevOps with AI-assisted work item creation
- [ ] Measure & report time saved to leadership

---

## ✅ AI Governance Checklist

- [ ] Never input client data, PII or confidential details into public AI tools
- [ ] Use Azure OpenAI Service for enterprise-grade privacy & compliance
- [ ] Establish a team AI usage policy before rollout
- [ ] Document AI tool usage in your project governance framework
- [ ] Review AI outputs before sharing with stakeholders — always

## 💡 Pro Tips from the Field

- **Start small** — one tool, one use case, prove the value
- **Prompt library is gold** — a shared team prompt library saves everyone time
- **AI is a co-pilot, not autopilot** — always review before you send
- **Use Azure OpenAI** for anything touching client or sensitive data
- **Celebrate wins** — share time saved with your team to drive adoption
