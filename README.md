## 🚀 Who Is This For?

- Project Managers working on **Azure Cloud & digital transformation** projects
- Agile teams looking for **ready-to-use sprint & scrum templates**
- PMs exploring **AI automation** in their daily workflows
- Anyone who wants **enterprise-grade PM templates** without starting from scratch

---

## ⭐ How to Use

1. Browse to the folder relevant to your need
2. Open the `.md` template file
3. Copy the content into your tool of choice (Notion, Confluence, Word, etc.)
4. Customise for your project and ship!
