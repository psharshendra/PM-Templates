## 🔁 Retro Formats to Keep It Fresh

| Format | Best For |
|---|---|
| **Start / Stop / Continue** | Quick and simple |
| **4Ls: Liked, Learned, Lacked, Longed For** | Deeper reflection |
| **Mad / Sad / Glad** | Team morale focus |
| **Sailboat** | Visual thinkers |
| **5 Whys** | Root cause deep dives |

---

## 💡 Facilitator Tips

- Create a **safe space** — what's said in retro stays in retro
- Focus on **process, not people** — never blame individuals
- **Timebox** each section strictly
- Limit to **3 action items** — quality over quantity
- **Follow up** on previous retro actions first
- Rotate **retro formats** to keep energy high
- End on a **positive note** always
