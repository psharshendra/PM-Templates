# 🌅 Daily Standup Format

A structured format for running effective, time-boxed daily standups.

---

## ⏱️ Standup Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Duration** | 15 minutes maximum |
| **Time** | Same time every day |
| **Format** | Standing up — keeps it focused |
| **Purpose** | Sync, not problem-solving |
| **Facilitator** | Scrum Master or rotating team member |

---

## 🔄 The 3 Core Questions

Each team member answers only these 3 questions:

### 1. ✅ What did I complete yesterday?
> Focus on **done** work, not in-progress
> Example: *"Completed the Azure RBAC configuration and raised PR #42"*

### 2. 🎯 What will I complete today?
> Specific tasks tied to sprint goal
> Example: *"Will finish the stakeholder comms draft and review John's PR"*

### 3. 🚧 Do I have any blockers?
> Flag blockers only — resolve offline
> Example: *"Blocked on access to the Azure subscription — need approval from [Name]"*

## 💡 Standup Anti-Patterns to Avoid

| ❌ Anti-Pattern | ✅ Better Approach |
|---|---|
| Status reporting to the PM | Team syncing with each other |
| Problem-solving in standup | Park it — resolve offline |
| Running over 15 minutes | Timebox strictly, take it offline |
| Skipping when busy | Non-negotiable — 15 mins daily |
| Vague updates ("working on stuff") | Specific task-level updates |

---

## 🔁 Remote Standup Tips

- Use **video on** to maintain engagement
- Use a **virtual timer** visible to all
- Rotate **facilitation** to keep energy up
- Use **Slack/Teams async standup** if timezones don't align
- Capture blockers in **Azure DevOps** immediately after

---

## 📊 Weekly Standup Health Check

Ask yourself at the end of each week:

- [ ] Did standups run under 15 minutes every day?
- [ ] Were all blockers resolved within 24 hours?
- [ ] Is the team syncing or just reporting to me?
- [ ] Are updates tied to the sprint goal?
- [ ] Is anyone consistently silent or disengaged?
